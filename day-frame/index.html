Day Frame

Day, date, weather and your next event on a framed analog dial with a digital time.

Get Day Frame on the Connect IQ Store Free

Day Frame is an analog-digital hybrid for the working day. Baton hands run over a dark dial with a minute track, with two sub-dials on either side of the centre and a digital time at 6 o'clock.

On the face

  • Hour, minute and seconds hands. The minute hand moves every second, as on a real watch
  • Day and date windows under 12
  • Current weather and a top line: your next calendar event to start with
  • Two sub-dials with needles: battery days and steps to start with
  • Digital time with seconds, a phone-connected icon and a notification bell

What you can change

The top line and both sub-dials can be changed from your phone, and each can be switched off. The top line takes your next event, sunset, sunrise, temperature, heart rate, resting heart rate, Body Battery™ or stress. The sub-dials take battery, steps, Body Battery™, stress, intensity minutes, floors or sleep score.

Also in the phone settings: the colour and seconds on or off. The digital time follows your watch's 12 or 24 hour setting, and units follow the watch too. On a touch watch, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
