Day Wheel

A weekday wheel, two rings and a digital time around broad rounded hands.

Get Day Wheel on the Connect IQ Store Free

Day Wheel has a tool-watch feel. Broad rounded hands run over a dark dial, and a wheel at 3 o'clock lists the week around the date, with today marked.

On the face

  • Hour, minute and seconds hands. The minute hand moves every second, as on a real watch
  • Weekday wheel with the date in the middle, always Monday to Sunday
  • Two rings: steps toward your goal and Body Battery™ to start with
  • Digital time with seconds, a phone-connected icon and a notification bell
  • Two values below it: heart rate and battery days to start with

What you can change

Both rings and both bottom values can be changed from your phone, and each can be switched off. The rings take steps, battery, Body Battery™, stress, intensity minutes, floors or sleep score. The bottom values take heart rate, resting heart rate, distance, intensity minutes, respiration, VO2 max or battery days.

Also in the phone settings: the colour and seconds on or off. The digital time follows your watch's 12 or 24 hour setting, and units follow the watch too. On a touch watch, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
