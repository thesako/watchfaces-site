Full Tank

A big Body Battery™ gauge beside analog hands, for seeing how much you have left today.

Get Full Tank on the Connect IQ Store Free

Full Tank reads like a fuel gauge. A long curved scale on the left shows Body Battery™ with a pointer, and the value sits large beside it.

On the face

  • Hour, minute and seconds hands. The minute hand moves every second, as on a real watch
  • Curved Body Battery™ gauge with its value
  • Weekday and date, with resting heart rate under them to start with
  • Stress to the right of centre to start with
  • A bottom ring that fills with steps toward your goal, with your heart rate above the count

What you can change

The line under the date, the value right of centre and the bottom ring can be changed from your phone, and each can be switched off. The two values take sunset, sunrise, temperature, resting heart rate, stress or battery days, among others. The ring takes steps, battery, stress, intensity minutes, floors, sleep score or Pulse Ox.

Also in the phone settings: the colour and seconds on or off. Units follow your watch's setting. On a touch watch, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
