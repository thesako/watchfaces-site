Track Hybrid

Hands over a seven-segment time, in the colours of old CRT, VFD, Nixie and LED displays.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store. Free.

Track Hybrid puts outline analog hands over a seven-segment time. Its colour presets are named after the display technology they copy. Pick one and the whole face follows.

In 7 colours: Amber CRT, EL yellow-green, Yellow mono, VFD, Nixie, Red LED, EL blue-green.

On the face

  • Analog hands over a seven-segment time, with seconds in active mode
  • Top field: the date, or ISO week, a second time zone, your next event, sunrise, sunset, temperature or weather
  • Two band gauges inside the minute track, names curved along them: Body Battery™ and stress to start with
  • Four data fields: heart rate, battery days, temperature and notifications to start with
  • Footer, calories to start with

What you can change

Every field and gauge can be changed from your phone, and most can be switched off for a cleaner dial.

Also in the phone settings: 12 or 24 hour time or the watch's setting, a leading zero for 12 h, seconds on or off, the date style (TUE 14 OCT, 14 OCT TUE, TUE 10/14 or 2026-10-14), the always-on style (Rich, Minimal or Time only), units, the first day of the week and a second time zone. On a Venu 4, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
