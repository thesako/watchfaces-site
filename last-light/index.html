Last Light

A 24-hour sun bezel and the daylight you have left, on an olive analog dial.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store. Free.

Last Light is for planning a day outside. A 24-hour bezel shows day and night with a marker for the sun, and the dial counts down the daylight left. After sunset it counts the night instead.

On the face

  • Hour, minute and seconds hands. The minute hand moves every second, as on a real watch
  • 24-hour day and night bezel with a sun marker
  • Daylight left and the sunset time
  • Barometric pressure in hPa with eight hours of trend bars
  • A sub-dial with altitude and your ascent today to start with
  • Digital time with seconds, a phone-connected icon and a notification bell
  • Weekday and date, and a pill with heart rate to start with

What you can change

The right sub-dial and the bottom pill can be changed from your phone, and both can be switched off. The sub-dial takes altitude, temperature, heart rate, resting heart rate, Body Battery™, stress, battery days or calories. The pill takes heart rate, resting heart rate, distance, intensity minutes, floors, Pulse Ox, respiration or VO2 max.

Also in the phone settings: the colour and seconds on or off. The digital time follows your watch's 12 or 24 hour setting, and units follow the watch too. On a touch watch, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
