Week Grid

Seven days of steps in one strip, today highlighted, above a six-cell data grid.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store. Free.

The top of Week Grid is a bar for each of the last seven days, with today lit up. It shows steps, or intensity minutes or floors if you prefer.

In 4 colours: Original, Cyan, Lime, Mono.

On the face

  • Week strip of the last seven days
  • Date with the current temperature and conditions
  • Large time with outlined minutes and seconds in active mode
  • A ruled 3 + 3 grid: heart rate, steps, notifications, calories, distance and floors to start with
  • Battery with days left, percent or the icon only
  • Two curved side gauges: Body Battery™ and stress to start with

What you can change

Both gauges, the week strip, the weather line, all six cells and the battery display can be changed from your phone. Most can be switched off.

Also in the phone settings: 12 or 24 hour time or the watch's setting, a leading zero for 12 h, seconds on or off, the date style (TUE 14 OCT, 14 OCT TUE, TUE 10/14 or 2026-10-14), the always-on style (Rich, Minimal or Time only), units, the first day of the week and a second time zone. On a Venu 4, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
