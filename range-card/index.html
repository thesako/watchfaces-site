Range Card

A digital face with thirteen data slots around a big, clear time.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store. Free.

Range Card is laid out like a field data card. A light band across the top carries the date. Under the time sits a ruled grid of six cells, a small step-goal dial, a weather line with today's high and low, and a status line. Two thin gauges run up the left and right edges.

In 4 colours: Original, Cyan, Lime, Mono.

On the face

  • Time with solid hours, outlined minutes and seconds in active mode
  • Top band: the date, or military date-time, your next calendar event, weather, training status or ISO week
  • Status line (notifications to start with) and a weather line with the high and low
  • Step-goal dial with a progress ring
  • A 3 + 3 grid: heart rate, battery days, calories, floors, altitude and intensity minutes to start with
  • Footer, sunset to start with
  • Edge gauges with their names printed along them: Body Battery™ on the left, stress on the right

What you can change

All 13 slots can be set from your phone, and most can be left empty: activity, health, battery, weather and time data, depending on the slot and your watch.

Also in the phone settings: 12 or 24 hour time or the watch's setting, a leading zero for 12 h, seconds on or off, the date style (TUE 14 OCT, 14 OCT TUE, TUE 10/14 or 2026-10-14), the always-on style (Rich, Minimal or Time only), units, the first day of the week and a second time zone. On a Venu 4, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
