Gauge Glass

Skeleton hands over an LCD time, with four glass level gauges for your data.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store. Free.

Gauge Glass mixes two kinds of watch. Skeleton hands sweep over a tick ring, and behind them sits an LCD-style panel with a seven-segment time and a 14-segment date line. Four pill-shaped gauges fill up like the level glass on a tank.

In 4 colours: Original, Cyan, Lime, Mono.

On the face

  • Skeleton hands and a seconds hand over a tick ring
  • Seven-segment time with seconds and a 14-segment date line
  • Four level gauges: steps (% of goal), battery days left, Body Battery™ and stress to start with

What you can change

Each gauge and the date line can show a different reading, or nothing. The gauges take readings that fill a level: steps, floors, intensity minutes, Body Battery™, stress, Pulse Ox, sleep score, battery, battery days, rain chance or solar intensity. The date line shows the date, ISO week, a second time zone or your next calendar event.

Also in the phone settings: 12 or 24 hour time or the watch's setting, a leading zero for 12 h, seconds on or off, the date style (TUE 14 OCT, 14 OCT TUE, TUE 10/14 or 2026-10-14), the always-on style (Rich, Minimal or Time only), units, the first day of the week and a second time zone. On a Venu 4, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
