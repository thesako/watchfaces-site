Split Ring

Steps and battery fill a segmented ring around analog hands and a digital time.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store. Free.

Split Ring is built around one wide segmented ring. The left half fills with steps toward your goal and the right half with battery days, with the values printed beside it.

On the face

  • Hour, minute and seconds hands over a fine patterned dial. The minute hand moves every second, as on a real watch
  • Segmented ring: steps on the left and battery days on the right to start with
  • Temperature with the day's high and low
  • Digital time with seconds, a phone-connected icon and a notification bell
  • Weekday and date, and a pill with heart rate to start with

What you can change

Each side of the ring and the bottom pill can be changed from your phone, and each can be switched off. The ring takes steps, battery, Body Battery™, stress, intensity minutes, floors or sleep score. The pill takes heart rate, resting heart rate, intensity minutes, floors, respiration or VO2 max.

Also in the phone settings: the colour and seconds on or off. The digital time follows your watch's 12 or 24 hour setting, and units follow the watch too. On a touch watch, touch and hold a field to open the matching screen on the watch.

Settings are changed in the Connect IQ Store app or the Garmin Connect app on your phone. How settings, always-on mode and missing values work.
