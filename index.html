Watch faces for the Venu 4 and Instinct 3 AMOLED

Nine faces, free on the Connect IQ Store. Each one has a dimmed always-on view, and you choose most of what it shows from your phone.

  • Range Card

    A digital face with thirteen data slots around a big, clear time.

  • Signal Box

    A retro instrument-panel face with a heart-rate dial and segment bars.

  • Field Kit

    Sunrise, sunset, Body Battery™ and a large time on one screen, for days outside.

  • Conduit

    Your last four hours of heart rate as a bar chart, under a two-tone time.

  • Week Grid

    Seven days of steps in one strip, today highlighted, above a six-cell data grid.

  • Gold Rule

    A quiet, gold-accented digital face with two ruled rows of data.

  • Track Hybrid

    Hands over a seven-segment time, in the colours of old CRT, VFD, Nixie and LED displays.

  • Gauge Glass

    Skeleton hands over an LCD time, with four glass level gauges for your data.

  • Duo Dial

    Analog hands and an LCD window on one dial, like the analog-digital sports watches of the 80s and 90s.
